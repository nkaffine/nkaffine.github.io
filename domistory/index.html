Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Domistory does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Game data you enter, including player names, scores, and round history, is stored locally on your device.

2. How Data Is Used

Game data is used solely to:

  • Calculate running totals and standings
  • Display game highlights and statistics
  • Let you pick up a game where you left off

3. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell personal data
  • Use third-party advertising
  • Track users across apps or websites
  • Share data with data brokers

Game recaps are shared only when you choose to share them, using your device’s standard sharing options.

4. Data Deletion

Game data is stored on your device. Domistory does not currently allow you to delete individual games or players. Archiving a player hides them from the player list when creating new games, but does not remove their data. Uninstalling the app removes all Domistory data from your device.

If you have questions about your data, contact: domistory.game@gmail.com
