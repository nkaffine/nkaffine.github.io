Unfold Ink

Draw on one half. Watch it unfold.

Unfold Ink is a drawing app that mirrors your artwork across the fold, so what you draw on one side appears unfolded on the other.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Unfold Ink does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The drawings you create are stored locally on your device, in the app’s Documents folder. We do not receive or have access to your drawings.

2. How Data Is Used

Your drawings are used solely to:

  • Display and edit your artwork
  • Save your progress automatically
  • Show your recent drawings so you can switch between them
  • Share or export a drawing when you choose to

3. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell personal data
  • Use third-party advertising or analytics
  • Track users across apps or websites
  • Share data with data brokers

Unfold Ink does not upload your drawings to us or to any server. A drawing leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it, using your device’s standard sharing options (for example Messages, AirDrop, Mail, or Save to Files). What happens to a drawing after you share it is governed by the app or service you send it to.

4. Data Deletion

Drawings are stored on your device and you can delete any drawing from within the app. Uninstalling Unfold Ink removes all of its data from your device.

If you have questions about your data, contact: developer.nkaffine@gmail.com
